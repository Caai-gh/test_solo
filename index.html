Returning to the Web

A Reflection on the Past

It's been a long, long time since I've tried my hand at coding a webpage. In fact, the last time I did was during a media course at university, where we were tasked with creating a basic landing page with information about us. remember it being a lot of fun, but I also remember having to look things up constantly, and never really feeling like I had a grasp on any of it. I wish that my professor(s) had taken the time to research or develop something like the Odin Project for us, so that maybe we'd have a chance of retaining the information.

...Well, that and we could return to the project after the class had completed and actually continue learning.

Where Am I Going With This?

I guess I hope that now, as an adult and not as a young person trying to find their way in life, with no concept of how important things like coding could become in my life, the lessons I'm learning will stick, and someday bear fruit for me. The ideal would of course be access to remote work, perhaps building websites or something for other small creators and maintaining blogs, etc. Another option would be simply making and maintaining my own business site. I'm not delusional enough to believe I'll be at that point any time soon, or ever be able to compete with the people my age and older who have been coding since the first time their fingers graced a computer keyboard. But maybe, someday, I'll be able to be competent enough to scrape by. Maybe.
